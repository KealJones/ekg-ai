# v0.9.2 — Language / Blueprint Architecture Note

This checkpoint records a research direction rather than claiming a solved language milestone.

## Added doctrine

- Blueprints may become a typed **semantic/executable IR**, not merely action workflows.
- The same representation may serve several functions when this remains semantically clean.
- No dogma that there must be one layer: additional language/semantic IRs are allowed when they solve a concrete representational or efficiency problem.
- Target loop: `English -> Blueprint -> cognition/execution -> Blueprint -> English`.
- Parsing and natural-language realization may become learned durable procedures/capabilities produced by the same self-growing machinery that acquires other tools.
- Teacher interactions should provide supervision for both language comprehension and production.
- Research references to mine include OpenCog/AtomSpace, AMR, Fluid Construction Grammar, executable semantic parsing, and graph-to-text/text-to-graph systems.
